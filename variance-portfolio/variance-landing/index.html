Our story

A neighbourhood café with Hyderabad roots

Roast Lane started as a weekend pop-up in Banjara Hills. We opened our Jubilee Hills space in 2024 — same beans, same filter ritual, a little more room to breathe.

We source from Araku and Chikmagalur co-ops, roast in small batches, and keep the menu short so every cup is consistent.

“Come for the filter coffee. Stay because the Wi-Fi actually works.” — regulars, Road No. 36
